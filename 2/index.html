Everybody.WorldCo-founder and Creative Director 2016–2025

I keep starting companies on the same premise: what you need already exists, it's just in the wrong hands.

Everybody.World came from one question — what does a brand look like if it actually accounts for the people who sew the clothes and the material they're made from? We printed our costs on the label, never bought an ad, grew from a $100,000 seed to $6 million a year, and made what we believe was the first 100% recycled cotton. 1000.world is the consultancy. ENORME is bringing back Ettore Sottsass's Memphis-era industrial design, 500 telephones at a time. iris.market is the current one: use what you have to get what you need.

American ApparelGlobal Creative Director 2004–2015

I started at Freshjive with Rick Klotz, where I also made The Propagandist, the label's own publication. Then I joined American Apparel in my early twenties and ended up Global Creative Director, running the look across e-commerce, retail and wholesale with a team of sixty, while the company grew into a $750 million public company. High-waisted jeans, plunging v-necks, a certain unbothered nudity — for better or worse, that became the visual language of a decade.

About

I design systems of culture, identity, and participation. My work spans global brands, emerging technologies, and civic futures — from shaping the visual language of a generation to building new frameworks for how society organizes itself.
